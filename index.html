I am a recent Master's graduate in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Business from Gisma University of Applied Science - First Class Honors. Currently contributing as a volunteer Research Assistant on enhancing the DeepSeekMoE architecture with dynamic routing algorithms for efficient training, fine-tuning, and inference — work that is contributing to an ACM publication.

My expertise is built on deep expertise in machine learning, neural networks, NLP, deep learning, and LLMs. My specializations lie in developing end-to-end pipelines for training, fine-tuning, optimization techniques, and model evaluation methods for LLMs and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and also bringing developed models to the production environment with continuous learning.

My research interests lie in vertical co-design between high-level AI applications (RAG and agentic systems) and low-level software infrastructure to optimize computational resources in production environments.
